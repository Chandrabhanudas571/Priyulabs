Omnichannel Architecture

Engineered for Modern Garment Retail

Four fundamental retail pillars designed to solve multi-variant inventory complexity, custom alterations, and inventory depreciation.

Multi-Variant Matrix Engine

Instant billing across Size (XS-XXL), Fit (Slim, Regular, Relaxed), and Color variants under one parent design SKU. Eliminate duplicate barcode entries and manage variant trees in seconds.

Digital Alteration & Job-Slip Desk

Track tailor assignments, body measurements, fitting notes, and automated SMS alerts when garments are ready for pickup. Never lose track of customer trousers, blouses, or suits again.

Smart Dead-Stock Liquidation

Automated aging reports for seasonal lines with dynamic bundle pricing recommendations before End of Season Sale (EOSS). Free up trapped working capital before lines go out of style.

Omnichannel Store-to-Catalog Sync

Single real-time inventory ledger synced simultaneously across boutique floor, WhatsApp store catalog, and website. Prevent double-selling when a customer purchases the last Medium in-store.

Precision Modules

Tailored Specifically for Garment Brands

Specialized boutique tools engineered to manage hangtag printing, designer margins, fitting holds, and compliant GST slabs.

Custom Barcode & Tag Printing

Design and print customized apparel tags with care icons, wash instructions, MRP, and size-color matrix barcodes directly from new shipment manifests.

Seasonal Split & Collection Planner

Categorize inventory by Summer/Festive/Winter lines with auto-archival for previous season SKUs and structured multi-year collection tracking.

Designer Consignment & Split Margins

Handle multi-designer inventory splits, commission accounting, and weekly vendor payouts without manual spreadsheets or reconciliation disputes.

Trial-Room & Fitting Hold System

Place items on customer fitting hold directly on tablet POS with 30-minute auto-release triggers to prevent stock locking during busy evening hours.

VIP Clienteling & Wardrobe History

View customer size profiles, purchase timelines, and tailored style preferences for personalized WhatsApp upselling and new collection lookbooks.
