What We're Building For You

A purpose-built ecosystem designed to eliminate table wait times, prevent kitchen chaos, and boost dine-in profitability.

📱
Self-Order QR Table Menus & Instant UPI Customers scan the QR on their table, view interactive visual menus with chef specials, place orders directly to the kitchen, and pay with 1-click UPI. Zero waiter delays.
👨‍🍳
Live Kitchen Display System (KDS) & Captain App Orders instantly appear on chefs' touchscreens categorized by station (Grill, Fryer, Dessert). Waitstaff track live preparation timers and receive alert pings when food is ready.
🎂
Bakery Advance Cake Booking & Weight Scale Sync Manage advance custom fondant cake delivery dates, flavor customizations, customer photo references, and live Bluetooth weighing scale auto-charging per gram.
🏨
Central Kitchen ERP & Bar Recipe Inventory Track raw ingredient deductions down to grams and milliliters for cocktails. Automatic purchase orders when milk, cheese, or coffee bean thresholds hit red.
